# Obligations, contributions and submission documents

## Planning obligations and charges

| Item | What it is | How to estimate |
|---|---|---|
| Affordable housing (S106) | Share of homes transferred to a Registered Provider below market value | Local plan policy (Colchester: 30% on major sites — confirm in S106). RP pays ~50–65% of market value depending on tenure mix. Viability assessment (RICS) can justify less |
| S106 financial contributions | Education, health, open space, highways, monitoring fees | Read the S106; budget £10–20k per home if not yet known |
| CIL | Community Infrastructure Levy, £/m² GIA of new floorspace | Only if the council has a CIL charging schedule; check the council's CIL page and indexation |
| Biodiversity Net Gain | Statutory 10% uplift measured with the DEFRA metric | Mandatory for major developments. From 6 Aug 2026, non-major sites ≤0.2 ha without priority habitat are exempt. On-site habitat first, then off-site units, then statutory credits (most expensive). Needs ecologist + 30-year management plan |
| Essex Coast RAMS | Per-dwelling tariff for recreational impact on coastal habitat sites; whole of Colchester is in the zone | See live-figures.md |
| SuDS | Surface-water drainage approved by Essex County Council as Lead Local Flood Authority | Infiltration tests (BRE 365) decide between soakaways/permeable paving (cheap) and attenuation tanks (expensive) |
| Highways (S38/S278) | Adoption of new roads / works on public highway | Essex Highways; if roads stay private, a management company and service charge |

## Typical reserved-matters submission for 10–50 homes

- Application form, certificates, fee
- Site location plan, site layout / masterplan, house-type plans and elevations, street scenes, boundary treatments, materials schedule
- Landscaping scheme and management plan
- Design and Access Statement / compliance with the outline and design code
- Accommodation schedule (unit types, bedspaces, GIA vs NDSS), affordable housing plan (tenure-blind distribution)
- Parking plan (local parking standards), refuse strategy and tracking for refuse vehicles
- Drainage strategy (SuDS) and maintenance plan
- BNG metric, BNG plan / habitat management and monitoring plan
- Ecology update, arboricultural impact assessment and tree protection plan
- Energy / sustainability statement
- Any documents named in outline conditions

## Before construction (after RM)

Discharge pre-commencement conditions → S106 trigger payments → Building Regulations full plans approval or initial notice → warranty registration (NHBC/LABC/Premier) → utilities (water, power, connection offers can take 3–6 months) → highways agreements → construction phase plan (CDM 2015: client duties, principal designer, principal contractor).
