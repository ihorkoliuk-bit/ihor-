# Building Regulations items that shape a house design (England)

| Part | Topic | Design consequence for ZON homes |
|---|---|---|
| A | Structure | Structural engineer's calcs for frame, roof, foundations (see zon-structural-prelim for early sizing only) |
| B | Fire | Escape windows from first-floor habitable rooms (two-storey types), protected stair, cavity barriers in timber frame, boundary distances limit unprotected glazing near site boundaries |
| C | Site and moisture | Radon (check map), DPC/DPM, ground gas |
| E | Sound | Only between dwellings or internal floors/walls around bedrooms and bathrooms (Part E internal walls Rw 40) |
| F | Ventilation | Whole-house ventilation (dMEV or MVHR), commissioning |
| G | Water | 125 l/person/day (110 if planning condition), hot-water safety |
| H | Drainage | Foul and surface water, soakaway rules |
| K | Protection from falling | Balustrades, stairs (pitch ≤ 42°, rise 150–220, going ≥ 220) |
| L | Conservation of fuel and power | Today: Part L 2021 notional dwelling. From 24 Mar 2027: Future Homes Standard — low-carbon heat (heat pump) by default, PV, improved fabric; transitional period for sites already commenced (check the regulations) |
| M | Access | M4(1) minimum; M4(2) often required by planning condition; NDSS by local plan |
| O | Overheating | Limits glazing area on south/west faces and needs cross-ventilation or dynamic modelling — large glazed gables trigger it |
| P | Electrical safety | |
| Q | Security | PAS 24 doors and accessible windows |
| R | Gigabit broadband | Infrastructure and connection |
| S | EV charging | One charge point per new dwelling with associated parking |

Warranty providers (NHBC, LABC Warranty, Premier) add their own technical standards — e.g. NHBC Chapter 4.2 foundation depths near trees on shrinkable clay. Without a 10-year warranty buyers cannot get a mortgage, so warranty sign-off is a sales condition, not optional.
