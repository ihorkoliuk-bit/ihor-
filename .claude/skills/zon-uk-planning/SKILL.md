# UK planning and delivery route

The aim is to tell the user, for a specific site and scheme, what is allowed, what is required, what it costs and in what order — and to mark which points are law, which are local policy, and which need the council's written view.

Always separate:
- **[Точно]** what statute or a document the user supplied says;
- **[Ймовірно]** typical practice / local policy that must be confirmed for this site;
- **[Припущення]** gaps you filled.

Planning rules change. Before relying on a fee, tariff or date, check `references/live-figures.md` for its "checked" date; if older than ~6 months or the decision is material, search the web for the current figure and update that file.

## Workflow for a site question

1. Get the decision notice (permission reference, description of development, conditions) and the S106 if one exists. The **description of development** governs what can be approved later — read it word for word.
2. Pick the route with `references/consent-routes.md`. The common trap: a change that alters the description (e.g. "bungalows" → two-storey houses, more units, a café) cannot go through reserved matters or S73; it needs a new full application.
3. List obligations and costs with `references/obligations.md` (affordable housing, CIL, S106 contributions, BNG, RAMS, SuDS, highways).
4. List the reports and drawings to submit (same file).
5. Set out the programme with statutory periods and realistic durations.
6. Flag Building Regulations and warranty items that change the design or cost now (`references/building-regs.md`) — e.g. heat pumps under the Future Homes Standard, Part M4(2), Part O overheating, fire separation.

## Output

A short memo: route → obligations with £ estimates → submission list → programme → open questions for the planning consultant or council (pre-app). Keep each claim tagged with its confidence. Say plainly when the scheme as proposed does not fit the consent.
