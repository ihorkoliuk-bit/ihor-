# Characteristic loads and typical build-ups (UK, low-rise housing)

Imposed (EN 1991-1-1, UK NA):
- Domestic floors (cat. A): qk 1.5 kN/m²; stairs 2.0; balconies 2.5.
- Roofs not accessible except for maintenance: 0.6 kN/m² (pitch < 30°), 0 above 60°, linear between; take the larger of this and snow.
- Snow (EN 1991-1-3, UK NA): ground snow sk depends on zone and altitude. North-east Essex at low altitude is typically around 0.4–0.5 kN/m²; roof snow = μ1 × sk with μ1 = 0.8 for 0–30°, reducing to 0 at 60°. Check the zone map for the site altitude.
- Wind (EN 1991-1-4): site-specific; governs racking of timber frame, roof uplift and fixings — leave to the engineer, but flag large glazed gables (they reduce racking walls).

Permanent (typical, kN/m² on plan):
- Timber joisted floor with boards + plasterboard ceiling: 0.40–0.50
- Pitched roof, tiles/slate on battens, rafters, insulation, plasterboard: 0.75–0.90 (on slope)
- Standing-seam metal roof build-up: 0.35–0.50 (on slope)
- In-roof PV adds ~0.15
- Timber-frame external wall with cladding: 0.6–0.9 kN/m² of wall; brick slip/brick outer leaf 2.0–2.3

Combinations:
- ULS (EN 1990 6.10): 1.35 Gk + 1.5 Qk (UK NA also permits 6.10a/b, which can be less onerous).
- SLS characteristic for deflection and bearing: Gk + Qk.

Typical member sizes to sanity-check engineer drawings (C24, 400 c/c, domestic floor):
47×170 ≈ 3.8 m, 47×195 ≈ 4.2 m, 47×220 ≈ 4.6 m clear span. Larger spans → glulam, I-joists or steel.
